AKURU INSTITUTE LOGO FILES
==========================

The logo is in the brand palette (docs/BRAND.md), as vectors:

  akuru-logo.svg          wine wordmark, gold mark — on white or the site beige
  akuru-logo-on-dark.svg  white wordmark, brand-gold mark — on the wine gradients
  akuru-logo-white.svg    one colour — any dark background
  akuru-mark.svg          the mark alone, gold
  akuru-logo-800.png      raster copy for email, structured data and anywhere SVG is not taken

The Blade component `<x-akuru-logo variant="default|on-dark|white" />` picks
the file. File names are lowercase and exact: a Linux server does not find
`akuru-logo.PNG` when asked for `akuru-logo.png`.

These were traced from a 200-pixel original. They are sharp on screen at any
size; for print or signage, replace them with vectors exported from the
original design file, keeping the same names.
